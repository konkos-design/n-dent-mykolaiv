About Sunny Nagar

The
story

From a small shop in India to a busy chair in Surrey. Two decades of cutting hair, and still obsessed with the details.

Since the early days

A beard isn't just hair. It's a statement.

Sunny began his hairstyling journey at just 13 and was soon running a small shop back in India, where friends travelled hours just to sit in his chair. Today the Nagar Hairstyle name has chairs in Surrey, Brampton and India, and Sunny himself is the barber many artists from the Punjabi music industry trust with their look.

The Surrey shop on 72 Ave is where it all comes together: a calm, clean space, a team that listens first, and cuts for men, women and kids.

Listen first

Every cut starts with a conversation about your face shape, your hair and how you wear it day to day.

Detail obsessed

Clean lines, sharp blends and beards shaped by hand. Clients come back for consistency.

Feel at home

Tea, coffee or water while you wait, and a team that makes kids and first-timers comfortable.
